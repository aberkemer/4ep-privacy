Privacy Policy — 4EP Approvals

Last updated: 27 August 2026

4EP Approvals is an app for documenting and controlling the approval of

purchase requests, purchase orders, invoices and contracts using the

four-eyes principle. This policy explains what the app stores, why, and

what control you have over it.

Who is responsible

4EP Approvals is provided by Alexander Berkemer, Darmstadt, Germany, as

a free, non-commercial project.

Contact for any privacy question, including data access, correction or

erasure requests: a.berkemer@gmail.com

Enquiries are answered by email. If you need a postal address for a

formal legal request, ask by email and one will be provided.

What the app stores

Account information. Your name, email address and a member ID that

is generated when you register. This identifies you to the other members

of your organization and attributes the approval decisions you make.

Requests you create. The description, vendor or payee, amount and

currency of each request, plus any photo or PDF you attach to it.

Approval records. Every submission, approval, rejection, return,

withdrawal and payment, together with who performed it and when. These

records are the purpose of the app: a four-eyes approval process is only

meaningful if it produces an accurate, tamper-resistant history.

Organization membership. Which organizations you belong to and the

role you hold in each.

Device token for notifications. If you allow notifications, an Apple

Push Notification token for your device, so the app can tell you when a

request needs your attention or yours has been approved. You can turn

notifications off at any time in iOS Settings.

Language preference. Stored only on your own device, not on the

server.

What the app does not do

  • No advertising, and no data is shared with advertisers.
  • No tracking across other apps or websites.
  • No analytics or behavioural profiling.
  • Your data is never sold, and is never shared with anyone outside your

own organization.

Where the data is stored

Data is stored using Supabase (Postgres database, authentication and

file storage), which acts as our data processor. Attachments are held in

private storage that requires an authenticated request to access.

Communication between the app and the server is encrypted with HTTPS.

Push notifications are delivered through **Apple Push Notification

service**. Only the request reference, type and status are included in a

notification — never amounts or attachments.

Who can see your data

Only members of the same organization. Access is enforced by row-level

security in the database, not just in the app, so a member of one

organization cannot read another organization's records. Within an

organization, what you can see and do depends on your role.

Legal basis and retention (GDPR)

Processing is based on the contract with your organization and its

legitimate interest in a documented approval process (Art. 6(1)(b) and

(f) GDPR).

Approval records are retained for as long as your organization needs

them for accounting and audit purposes. This is why deleting your

account anonymizes rather than erases your history — see below.

Deleting your account

You can delete your account at any time in the app: **Account → Delete

Account**.

When you do:

  • Your name and email address are removed.
  • Your login is disabled permanently and you cannot sign in again.
  • Your organization memberships and notification tokens are deleted.
  • Requests and approvals you were involved in remain in your

organization's records, attributed only to your member ID and no

longer to your name or email.

Approval history is kept because removing it would destroy the record of

decisions other people relied on, and because organizations are

typically required to retain such records. If you need your data erased

entirely, contact us by email and we will assess the request

against those retention obligations.

Your rights

Under GDPR you have the right to access, correct, erase and export your

personal data, to restrict or object to processing, and to complain to a

supervisory authority. You can view and correct your name and email in

the app under Account. For anything else, contact us by email.

Children

The app is not directed at children and is not intended for use by

anyone under 16.
