Privacy Policy — 4EP Approvals

Last updated: 30 September 2026

4EP Approvals is an app for documenting and controlling the approval of purchase requests, purchase orders, invoices and contracts using the four-eyes principle. This policy explains what the app stores, why, and what control you have over it. It covers both the iOS app and the web app at the same address.

Who is responsible

4EP Approvals is provided by Alexander Berkemer, Darmstadt, Germany, as a free, non-commercial project.

Contact for any privacy question, including data access, correction or erasure requests: a.berkemer@gmail.com

Enquiries are answered by email. If you need a postal address for a formal legal request, ask by email and one will be provided.

What the app stores

Account information. Your name, email address and a member ID that is generated when you register. This identifies you to the other members of your organization and attributes the approval decisions you make.

Sign-in with Google or Apple. In the web app you can sign in with your Google or Apple account instead of a password. If you do, Google or Apple tells us your name, your email address and an account identifier; we do not receive your password or any other data from those accounts, and we do not grant them access to anything in 4EP Approvals. With Apple you may choose to hide your email address, in which case we receive an Apple relay address instead. Signing in this way is optional — an email address and password work just as well.

Requests you create. The description, vendor or payee, amount and currency of each request, plus any photo or PDF you attach to it.

Approval records. Every submission, approval, rejection, return, withdrawal and payment, together with who performed it and when. These records are the purpose of the app: a four-eyes approval process is only meaningful if it produces an accurate, tamper-resistant history.

Organization membership. Which organizations you belong to and the role you hold in each.

Device token for notifications. If you allow notifications, a token identifying your device or browser, so the app can tell you when a request needs your attention or yours has been approved. On iOS this is an Apple Push Notification token; in a browser it is a Web Push subscription. You can turn notifications off at any time in iOS Settings, or in your browser's site settings.

Language preference. Stored only on your own device, not on the server.

What the app does not do

  • No advertising, and no data is shared with advertisers.
  • No tracking across other apps or websites.
  • No analytics or behavioural profiling.
  • Your data is never sold, and is never shared with anyone outside your own organization.

Where the data is stored

Data is stored using Supabase (Postgres database, authentication and file storage), which acts as our data processor. Attachments are held in private storage that requires an authenticated request to access. Communication between the app and the server is encrypted with HTTPS.

If you choose to sign in with Google or Apple, that sign-in is handled by Google (Google Ireland Limited) or Apple (Apple Distribution International Ltd.) under their own privacy policies; they learn that you signed in to 4EP Approvals.

Push notifications are delivered through the Apple Push Notification service on iOS, or the W3C Web Push standard in a browser. A notification contains the request's description, reference, type and what happened to it, plus the text of a note or return comment where there is one — never attachments.

Emails are sent through Brevo (Sendinblue SAS, France): the optional email when a request awaits your approval (which you switch on yourself under Account), and — if an administrator of your organization sets it up — archive emails with request records, their attachments and audit-log extracts to addresses your organization chooses.

The web app is hosted on Cloudflare Pages, which processes your IP address to deliver the site.

Who can see your data

Only members of the same organization. Access is enforced by row-level security in the database, not just in the app, so a member of one organization cannot read another organization's records. Within an organization, what you can see and do depends on your role.

Legal basis and retention (GDPR)

Processing is based on the contract with your organization and its legitimate interest in a documented approval process (Art. 6(1)(b) and (f) GDPR).

Approval records are retained for as long as your organization needs them for accounting and audit purposes. This is why deleting your account anonymizes rather than erases your history — see below.

Deleting your account

You can delete your account at any time in the app: Account → Delete Account.

When you do:

  • Your name and email address are removed.
  • Your login is disabled permanently and you cannot sign in again.
  • Your organization memberships and notification tokens are deleted.
  • Requests and approvals you were involved in remain in your organization's records, attributed only to your member ID and no longer to your name or email.

Approval history is kept because removing it would destroy the record of decisions other people relied on, and because organizations are typically required to retain such records. If you need your data erased entirely, contact us by email and we will assess the request against those retention obligations.

Your rights

Under GDPR you have the right to access, correct, erase and export your personal data, to restrict or object to processing, and to complain to a supervisory authority. You can view and correct your name and email in the app under Account. For anything else, contact us by email.

Children

The app is not directed at children and is not intended for use by anyone under 16.
